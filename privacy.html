Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LA Speed Alert (the “App”), published by coywon (“we”), handles information. It applies to the current version of the Android App as described below.

1. Location access and on-device processing

When you start driving monitoring or use the map’s location features, the App receives device location, including GPS or network-derived position, speed and accuracy, to determine proximity to listed safety-camera locations, distance and direction, and to issue alerts. These calculations occur on your device. The App does not directly upload your raw GPS coordinates to our servers, create a location-history account, or store a route history. Location values used for monitoring are held in device memory rather than recorded as a location history.

2. Monitoring when the App is not visible

Driving monitoring starts when you select “Start monitoring.” It uses an Android location foreground service with an ongoing notification, so monitoring can continue when you leave the App or swipe it away from the recent-apps screen. Android may restart this service after a system-initiated termination, allowing monitoring to resume. You can stop monitoring using the App’s Stop control or the notification’s Stop action, and you can revoke location permission in Android Settings. The current App does not request Android's separate ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION permission.

3. Map imagery and indirect location disclosure

To show maps, the App requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap at tile.openstreetmap.org. The requested tile coordinates identify the map region being viewed. When the map is centered on your current position, those requests can indirectly disclose your approximate location or map-view area to the map server, with finer detail at higher zoom levels. Network requests also disclose technical connection data such as your IP address, request time, and the App's User-Agent. We do not directly include raw GPS latitude/longitude or advertising identifiers in these tile requests. Map tiles may be cached on your device (up to approximately 50 MB). See the OSM Foundation Privacy Policy for information about its handling of information.

4. Public road and camera data

The App downloads camera and road-segment reference data over HTTPS from a City of Los Angeles ArcGIS-hosted service (services.arcgis.com). These requests are for public reference datasets and do not include your GPS coordinates in the request parameters. The service provider will nonetheless receive ordinary network metadata such as your IP address, time of request, and User-Agent. The App may cache downloaded reference data on your device.

5. Locally stored information

The App stores certain preferences and operational information locally, such as language, alert-display duration, warning options, developer mode, and a last-error message. It also stores cached map tiles and reference data. The App does not currently offer accounts, login, purchases, cloud backup, or a personal driving-history feature. You can remove locally saved information by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it. This does not erase records that external servers independently maintain.

6. Anonymous usage counts (for versions with this feature enabled)

Some upcoming versions of the App may send limited usage events to our server at coywon.com to understand overall App usage. The three event types are app_open (new app launch), monitoring_start (user-initiated monitoring start), and camera_alert (a genuine camera alert; test alerts excluded). The event request body contains only the event name. It does not include GPS coordinates, travel routes, speed, camera identifiers, account identifiers, advertising IDs, Android device identifiers, or an App-generated user ID.

Where this feature is enabled, the server processes accepted events into daily totals by event type and UTC date. It does not create per-user histories or store individual events in the statistics database. Network connections nevertheless expose an IP address and technical request metadata to server infrastructure. Rate limiting may process the IP address temporarily in memory; web-server or security error logs may retain IP addresses and request metadata for up to 14 days. We use these limited counts for service analytics, capacity planning and basic abuse prevention. The statistics feature has no separate in-app opt-out switch; uninstalling the App stops future event transmissions. Because events are not linked to a user account or persistent identifier, individual submissions cannot generally be located or deleted from aggregate counts.

This section describes a feature under development and applies only when the App version you are using actually sends those usage events. Versions that do not include the event-sending feature do not transmit these events. Before enabling it publicly, we will verify the production server configuration and update Google Play disclosures as necessary.

8. Advertising and third-party analytics SDKs

The App does not currently include advertising, Firebase, or third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. It is free. We may introduce banner advertising in a future version. If new SDKs are added, we will update this Policy and any required Google Play disclosures before releasing that version.

7. Data sharing and security

We do not operate an App backend that receives GPS histories, and we do not sell your raw location history. As described above, requests to OpenStreetMap and the ArcGIS-hosted public-data service expose relevant network metadata to those service operators; map-tile requests may indirectly reveal the viewed area. The App uses HTTPS for its network requests. No network service can promise absolute security.

9. Children

The App is designed for drivers and is not directed to children.

10. Your choices and requests

You can stop monitoring in the App or from its persistent notification, revoke location access through Android settings, and clear App storage or uninstall it. Because the current App does not maintain accounts or server-side location histories, there is no App account or stored driving history for us to delete. For privacy questions, contact us at coywon@gmail.com.

11. Changes

We may revise this Policy as App functionality or relevant practices change. The effective date above will be updated when we publish changes.
